Billing & refunds

  • Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play. You can manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
  • Canceling a subscription does not automatically refund it. For refund requests, email us: reports that were generated and delivered are non-refundable; if generation failed you are refunded automatically.

Common questions

  • How do I delete my account? In the app (Settings), or on this site: delete without the app.
  • Is this app accurate? Yarrow is built for entertainment and self-reflection. It is not a prediction of the future and not professional advice.
  • Which languages are available? English first; other languages appear in the app only after they pass our quality review.
