Delete your account

You can delete your Yarrow account and personal data in two ways:

Option 1 — in the app (recommended)
Open Yarrow → Settings → Delete account and confirm.
Option 2 — on this page, without the app

Enter the account ID shown inside the app (Settings → Delete account → the code displayed on that screen). We use it to locate your data — do not send us your password or birth details.





This web form submits your deletion request to the same backend the app uses. Your personal data is then removed within 7 days, and you can return to this page and re-enter the same account ID to check the request status.

Before you delete

  • Cancel your subscription first, in Google Play: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Yarrow → Cancel (open your Play subscriptions). Deleting the account does not cancel a renewal on its own.
  • Deleting removes your data permanently and cannot be undone. If you want a copy first, use Settings → Export my data in the app, or ask us (§ Questions below).

What happens next

  • Your personal data is deleted within 7 days.
  • Deleted: birth details, name/nickname, gender, email (if provided), journal entries, your card and report history, app settings, notification token.
  • Kept (with identifying details stripped): anonymous accounting records we are legally required to keep (up to 7 years), and anti-fraud records.
