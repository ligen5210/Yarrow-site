Delete your account

You can delete your Yarrow account and personal data in two ways:

Option 1 — in the app (recommended, self-serve)
Open Yarrow → Settings → Delete account and confirm. Your account is marked for deletion immediately, your personal data is removed, and any active membership is revoked. The deletion cannot be undone after a 7-day grace period.
Option 2 — by email (if you cannot use the app)

Email yifei4trade@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion request”.

If you still have the app installed, please first open Settings → Export my data and attach the exported file to your email — it lets us locate your record precisely. If you no longer have the app, include anything that helps us identify your purchase, for example the Google Play order number (format GPA.xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxxx) from your Play purchase receipt, and the approximate date you installed the app.

We action email requests within 7 days and confirm to you by reply.

Before you delete

  • Cancel your subscription first, in Google Play: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Yarrow → Cancel (open your Play subscriptions). Deleting the account revokes access but does not cancel a Play renewal on its own.
  • Deleting removes your data permanently and cannot be undone. If you want a copy first, use Settings → Export my data in the app.

What happens next

  • Your personal data is deleted within 7 days.
  • Deleted: birth details, name/nickname, gender, email (if provided), journal entries, your card and report history, app settings, notification token.
  • Kept in de-identified form only: accounting records we are legally required to keep (up to 7 years; payment tokens and receipts are stripped), and anonymized safety events.
