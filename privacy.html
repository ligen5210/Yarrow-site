Privacy Policy

App: Yarrow · Website: libot.store · Version 2.0 · Effective: 2026-10-17 · This English version is the master text. A Chinese reference translation is published at privacy.zh.html.

In short: we collect the birth details you type and the content you create, we use them to generate your chart and readings, your account data is stored in the European Union, and the text you ask us to generate is processed by an AI provider in China. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. You can export or delete your data at any time.

Yarrow (the app and this website) is operated by HAINAN YIFEI TRADING COMPANY LIMITED, a company established in China ("we", "us"). For the purposes of the EU/UK GDPR and similar laws, we are the data controller of the personal data described below.

Contact for all privacy matters: yifei4trade@gmail.com. We answer privacy requests within 30 days.

2. Who may use Yarrow (18+)

Yarrow is intended for adults aged 18 or over. When you first open the app we show an age screen and ask you to confirm that you are 18 or older; if you decline, the app does not continue. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used Yarrow, contact us and we will delete the account.

4. How your readings are produced (AI processing)

Your chart is calculated deterministically by our own engine — the pillars, elements and cycles are never invented by an AI. The wording of your readings and report is generated with the help of an artificial-intelligence service operated by our provider DeepSeek (Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Co., Ltd., China).

What we send to the AI provider when you ask for a reading, a report or an interpretation:

  • chart facts derived from your birth details — for example your core element, the four pillar symbols, the balance of elements, your archetype card, the current life-cycle chapter and the current year;
  • the question or context text you typed (for example your Journal question or your "burning question").

What we do not send to the AI provider: your name or nickname, your email address, your raw date of birth, birth time or birthplace as such, your purchase records, or your device identifiers. Note that the chart facts above are derived from your birth details and therefore relate to you.

AI-generated text can be wrong, generic or unexpected. Every AI-generated part of the app is marked as AI-generated, and the app includes a report function so you can flag AI content you believe is wrong or harmful. Reports and readings are for entertainment and self-reflection only; they are not medical, psychological, financial or legal advice, and they are not predictions of your future.

5. International transfers (including transfers to China)

Your account data is stored in the European Union (see §6). However, generating your readings requires sending the data described in §4 to our AI provider in China, and our operator is established in China. Because China has no EU adequacy decision, that transfer requires specific safeguards.

Current mechanism. Today, the transfer of the §4 data to China happens only when you actively ask us to generate a reading, report or interpretation, and it is covered by:

  • your explicit consent (Article 49(1)(a) GDPR; and for the UK, the equivalent provision of the UK GDPR) — which you give by choosing to generate AI content after reading this section; and
  • the performance of our contract with you (Article 49(1)(b) GDPR) — the AI-generated reading is the very thing you asked for.

These are "derogations" intended for transfers that are not systematic, so we are working to put in place a stronger, long-term mechanism: a data-processing agreement and Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914) with our AI provider. We will update this policy when that is in place.

You are in control. You can use the app without AI generation (chart, cards and calculations are produced by our engine) — simply do not start a reading, a report or an interpretation. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us; withdrawing means we can no longer generate AI readings for you. Before we send anything to the AI provider, the app and your action indicate that an AI-generated reading is being requested.

6. Where your data is stored

Your account data (profile, birth details, chart, your Journal, delivered reports, entitlement records) is stored in a managed database hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). App files and share images are produced on your device. Payments are processed by Google Play; we never receive your card details.

7. How long we keep it

  • While your account is active: we keep your data so the app works for you.
  • When you request deletion: your account is deactivated and your personal data is deleted from our production system within 7 days (our deletion workflow clears your profile, birth details, charts, Journal, reports and AI conversation records).
  • Exceptions: purchase and tax records are kept for up to the period required by accounting law (currently up to 7 years) but stripped of identifying details; minimal records may be kept where necessary to prevent fraud or to defend legal claims.
  • Safety records: if you used a feature where you expressed thoughts of self-harm, we record a confidential safety event so we can keep the crisis response working. This record does not contain your journal or your message text.

About that safety record: information about a person's mental state can be a "special category" of personal data under the GDPR. We process this record only (a) to protect your vital interests where you may be at risk (Article 9(2)(c) GDPR) and (b) to verify that our crisis response works. We keep the record in a restricted form (the fact that a safety response was triggered, when, in which feature and in which language — not the text you wrote), and you can ask us to delete it with your account.

8. Your rights

You can exercise the following rights using the in-app tools (Settings → Export my data / Delete my account) or by email:

  • Access — get a copy of your data (in-app export delivers a machine-readable file).
  • Rectification — correct your birth details, name or settings in the app.
  • Erasure — delete your account and personal data.
  • Restriction and objection — ask us to limit or stop certain processing.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, without affecting processing carried out before you withdrew it.
  • Complaint — to your local data-protection authority (EU: your national supervisory authority; UK: the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk).

We apply the GDPR standard to everyone, wherever you live. We do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

9. Your crisis safety information

If you tell the app about thoughts of self-harm or suicide, the app does not send that text to any AI service: it stops and shows a fixed safety card with a phone button, and it records the event so we can check that the response worked. The card lists crisis lines for your region, for example:

  • United States — 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988.
  • United Kingdom — Samaritans: 116 123 (24/7, free); Shout text line: text SHOUT to 85258.
  • Canada — 9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline: call or text 988.
  • Australia — Lifeline: 13 11 14.
  • Germany — TelefonSeelsorge: 0800 111 0 111.
  • Sweden — Mind Självmordslinjen: 90101.
  • Poland — 116 123 (24/7).
  • European Union — 116 123 emotional-support line (available in many, not all, EU countries); emergency 112.

The full, current list is maintained by our operations team and updated without an app release. Yarrow is not a crisis service and cannot help you in an emergency.

10. Not medical, financial or legal advice

Yarrow is an entertainment and self-reflection product. Nothing in the app is medical, psychological, financial, investment or legal advice, and nothing in it is a prediction or guarantee of future events. Do not rely on it for decisions where a professional is required.

11. Security

Access to your account data is restricted by database-level rules so that one account cannot read another's records. We use encrypted transport (HTTPS) and store data in a managed EU-hosted database. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affecting your rights occurs, we will notify you and the competent authority as required.
