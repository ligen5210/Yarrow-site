Privacy Policy

App: Yarrow · Site: libot.store · Effective: 2026-10-10 (English version; localized versions pending review)

1. Who we are

This site and the Yarrow app are operated by HAINAN YIFEI TRADING COMPANY LIMITED, established in China (legal entity details available on request via the contact in §10).

2. What we collect

  • Birth details you enter (date, time if provided, birthplace) — used to generate your chart, card, and readings.
  • Gender (you may select "prefer not to say") and an optional first name or nickname.
  • Context you choose to share in the app: occupation, relationship status, and the question you want to ask (all optional).
  • Your journal entries, if you use the journal feature (free-form text you write).
  • Email address, only if you choose to upgrade your anonymous account to an email account.
  • Purchase and subscription records (processed by Google Play; we store the order state needed to grant your entitlements — we never see or store your card details).
  • Push notification token, if you enable notifications.
  • App settings (language, theme, timezone, your 18+ confirmation result) and basic usage events (e.g. which screens are opened) to keep the app working and improve it.

We do not ask for your real name, phone number, contacts, or precise location, and we do not scan your photos. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

3. Why we process it

Only to provide the service you asked for: generating your personal chart and content, delivering purchases, remembering your settings, sending the notifications you opted into, and providing support. The legal bases are your consent and the performance of the service.

4. AI processing and cross-border transfers

Yarrow's readings and reports are generated with the help of artificial intelligence. When a reading or report is generated, your chart data (derived from the birth details you entered) is sent to our AI service provider, DeepSeek, to produce the text. The AI provider processes this data on our instructions and under its own data-processing terms. You can recognize AI-generated sections in the app; charts and calculations themselves are produced deterministically by our own engine and never leave our infrastructure.

Because our operator is established in China and our AI provider processes data there, using Yarrow involves transfers of your data to China, in addition to storage in the European Union described in §5. Where required by law, these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards and on your informed use of the service. EU/UK users can exercise the rights in §8 at any time.

5. Where it is stored

Your data is stored in a managed database hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt). As described in §4, AI-generated content involves processing in China. Payments are processed by Google Play; we never see or store your card details.

6. How long we keep it

For as long as your account is active. When you request deletion, personal data is removed within 7 days, except records we must keep for accounting (up to 7 years, as required by tax law) or fraud prevention, which are stripped of identifying details.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete your account inside the app (Settings), or on this web page without the app: Delete your account. Before deleting, remember to cancel any active subscription in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions). You may also contact us directly (§10).

8. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correction, or deletion at any time via the support email or the in-app tools. Where GDPR applies, you also have the right to restrict processing, to object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time (without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal) — and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. We apply the GDPR standard to everyone.

9. Children

Yarrow is intended for adults 18 and over and is not directed at children. We confirm age (18+) when you first use the app.
